# Privacy Policy for Nashe Video

**Effective date: 22 September 2026**

## 1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy explains how Best Choice Trade LTD (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you access or use the Nashe Video application, website, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

By using the Service, you acknowledge that your information will be processed as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please discontinue using the Service.

For the purposes of applicable data protection legislation, Best Choice Trade LTD is the data controller unless otherwise stated.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect the following categories of information.

### 2.1. Account Information

When you create or manage an account, we may collect:

- Your name, username, date of birth, and profile details;
- Your email address and telephone number;
- Your password in encrypted or hashed form;
- Your profile image and other information you choose to provide;
- Account verification and authentication information.

### 2.2. Content and Activity

We may process information relating to your activity within the Service, including:

- Videos, images, audio, descriptions, comments, and other content you upload or publish;
- Likes, subscriptions, favourites, searches, viewing history, and interactions;
- Playlists, channel information, and account settings;
- Messages and requests sent to our support team;
- Reports, complaints, and moderation-related information.

Content published through public features of the Service may be visible to other users and may be shared outside the Service.

### 2.3. Device and Technical Information

When you use the Service, we may automatically collect:

- IP address;
- Device type, model, operating system, and application version;
- Browser type and language settings;
- Mobile network and internet service provider;
- Device, advertising, installation, and session identifiers;
- Login times, pages or screens viewed, clicks, and session duration;
- Crash reports, performance data, and diagnostic information;
- Approximate location inferred from your IP address.

Where permitted by you and supported by your device, the Service may access the camera, microphone, media library, notifications, or precise location. You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

### 2.4. Information from Third Parties

If you sign in through a third-party authentication provider or connect the Service to another platform, we may receive information permitted by your settings with that provider, such as your name, email address, profile image, and external account identifier.

We may also receive information from analytics, advertising, security, hosting, payment, and technical service providers.

We may use information to:

- Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts;
- Provide video playback, uploading, publishing, commenting, subscriptions, and other Service functions;
- Personalise recommendations, search results, and content feeds;
- Remember settings and user preferences;
- Process payments or subscriptions, where applicable;
- Respond to questions, support requests, and complaints;
- Send important service, security, and account notifications;
- Send marketing communications where permitted by law or with your consent;
- Measure audience engagement and analyse Service performance;
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, spam, copyright violations, and security incidents;
- Moderate content and enforce our terms and community rules;
- Develop, test, maintain, and improve the Service;
- Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests;
- Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we process personal information on one or more of the following grounds:

- **Performance of a contract:** processing necessary to provide the Service you requested;
- **Legitimate interests:** operating, securing, analysing, and improving the Service, provided those interests do not override your rights;
- **Consent:** where you have given permission for specific processing, such as certain marketing, device permissions, or optional technologies;
- **Legal obligation:** processing required to comply with applicable law;
- **Protection of vital interests:** processing necessary to protect someone’s life or physical safety;
- **Legal claims and public interest:** where processing is permitted or required for these purposes.

You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

## 5. Recommendations and Automated Processing

We may use information about your viewing history, searches, subscriptions, likes, and other interactions to organise content and provide personalised recommendations.

These systems may automatically evaluate interests and viewing preferences. Unless we separately inform you, we do not use automated processing to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you.

We may share information with:

- Authentication, distribution, advertising, or integration partners where you use the relevant feature or have provided consent;

- Government authorities, courts, regulators, and law-enforcement bodies where disclosure is legally required;
- A buyer, investor, or successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, or sale of all or part of our business;

We require service providers processing personal information on our behalf to use appropriate safeguards and process the information only for authorised purposes.

We do not sell personal information for money. If applicable law treats certain advertising or analytics disclosures as a “sale” or “sharing,” you may have the right to opt out.

## 7. International Data Transfers

The Service and our providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as adequacy regulations, approved contractual clauses, contractual protections, and supplementary security measures.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Provide and maintain the Service;
- Keep your account active;
- Fulfil the purposes described in this Policy;
- Comply with legal, accounting, and reporting obligations;

- Prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

Retention periods depend on the type of information, the purpose of processing, and legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we delete or anonymise it. Some information may remain temporarily in secure backups or be retained where required by law or necessary for legitimate legal purposes.

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

You are responsible for keeping your login details confidential and for notifying us if you believe your account has been compromised.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

- Request access to your personal information;
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
- Request deletion of your information;
- Request restriction of processing;
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- Withdraw consent at any time;
- Request a portable copy of certain information;
- Object to direct marketing;
- Request information about international transfer safeguards;

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information through available account settings or by contacting us.

- Records necessary to resolve disputes or enforce agreements;
- Data temporarily retained in backups.

Where possible, we will delete or anonymise retained information when the relevant retention period ends.
